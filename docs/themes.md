# Pick a theme

1. Select the palette icon beside Settings on Home.
2. Press OK.
3. Choose a color, Auto or a festival.
4. Press Back to return Home.

## Everyday themes

Ocean Blue, Emerald, Violet, Crimson, Amber and Aurora are available. Auto uses a solid dark background between festivals, with normal greetings, day, date and time.

## Festival themes

There are 22 manual theme names for major Telugu Hindu celebrations, including Dussehra, Deepavali, Sankranti, Bhogi, Kanuma, Ugadi, Bathukamma, Navaratri, Bonalu and Vinayaka Chavithi. This is not an exhaustive religious calendar.

Manual themes work outside the event date. Auto uses bundled dates for 2026–2030. Multi-day periods include Sankranti (three days), Navaratri (nine), Deepavali (three) and Ganesh celebrations (nine). Dates depend on location and tradition; review `launcher/assets/festivals.json` for your region.

Festival effects are brief. Deepavali has fireworks; other celebrations have petals and themed decoration. The generated Durga wallpaper appears for Dussehra/Navaratri. The 2026 Dussehra entry ends on 20 October; Auto returns to normal afterward unless another dated event applies.

Calendar references: [Telangana 2026 calendar](https://www.telangana.gov.in/Downloads/calendar-2026/) and [Drik Panchang Telugu calendar](https://www.drikpanchang.com/telugu/calendar/telugu-calendar.html).
